# LoreOS

Answers from your organization's documents, with the sources to check them.

LoreOS is a self-hostable document Q&A engine: upload documents, ask questions, get answers that cite the passage they came from. Its focus is measured retrieval: every search change is scored against a hand-written question set, and the results are published here.

**Status:** planning. Nothing runs yet. The plan and its decisions are in [docs/sdd/v1/brief.md](docs/sdd/v1/brief.md).
